\begin{abstract}
Long-context Large Language Models (LLMs) operating over 200k--1M+ token windows suffer from quadratic $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ attention compute costs, severe ``Lost in the Middle'' retrieval decay, and destructive context compaction. Meanwhile, existing external agentic memory frameworks rely on iterative runtime LLM summarization loops or uncompressed floating-point vector stores that bottleneck on memory bandwidth and I/O latency. We present the \textbf{Bare-Metal Agentic Memory Architecture (BAMA)}, a hardware-software co-designed cortical substrate that enables 4B--9B parameter local models (\texttt{Gemma-4-E4B} and \texttt{Qwen-3.5-9B}) running on a single consumer workstation (AMD Ryzen 9 8940HX, 96\,GB DDR5, NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti 12\,GB VRAM) to execute pre-compiled domain reasoning and deterministic verification within a pinned 16k-token L1 execution register. BAMA introduces four core systems innovations: (1) a \textbf{90/10 Notebook Context Window (NBCW) Split} paired with \textbf{Dual-Cap B.I.F.R.O.S.T.} bin packing (\texttt{97.66\%} mean utilization across 46 content bins on the 10.35M-word BAMA Flagship corpus, and \texttt{139,764 words/sec} unique-word ingestion throughput---\texttt{uniqueWordsPacked = 26,211,318} / \texttt{ingestElapsedSec = 187.54\,s} in \texttt{BEACON\_HQ\_SUMMARY.json}, or \texttt{141,954 words/sec} for \texttt{totalBinWords = 26,622,069} / \texttt{187.54\,s} in \texttt{BIFROST\_BINS\_MANIFEST.json}---on the Beacon HQ corpus); (2) an offline \textbf{4-Tier Cortical Engine ($W_1$--$W_4$)} that compiles raw text into typed Epistemic Facts, Procedural DAGs, Negative Anti-Hallucination Traps, and Affective Anchors; (3) \textbf{Zero-Decompression Quadratic Golden Graph (\texttt{ZDC-QEGG})} 256-byte memory cells in the real golden integer ring $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi] \subset \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$, achieving \texttt{12.0$\times$} compression vs.\ Float32 (\texttt{6.0$\times$} vs.\ FP16; \texttt{20,480 bytes} for 80 kernels), \texttt{61.77\%} byte-read savings via $1/\varphi$ Lazy Child Bit-Plane Collapse, and \texttt{0.0608\,ms} (\texttt{60.8\,$\mu$s}) multi-modal Matryoshka LUT inner products; and (4) \textbf{GAAMA 2-Hop Personalized PageRank (PPR)} with degree-threshold hub dampening ($\text{damp}(d)=\min(1,\theta/d)$, $\alpha=0.15$, $\theta=50$), suppressing high-degree hubs by \texttt{50.50\%} at $d=101$ (\texttt{90.00\%} at $d=500$) and achieving a \texttt{102.0$\times$} target-to-hub score ratio (\texttt{0.180625} vs.\ \texttt{0.001771}) in \texttt{0.155\,ms}. Across an 8-tier evaluation on a 5-task author-written multi-part verification suite (15 deterministic sub-assertions modeled after MMLU-Pro, GPQA-Diamond, MATH-500, HumanEval+, and IFEval task structures), combining domain-primed 72-kernel BAMA with the deterministic \texttt{W3SelfVerificationTrap} (with zero verifier-prompt hints) elevates local 4B--9B mean accuracy from \texttt{26.66\%} (\texttt{@LUMEN} solo) and \texttt{40.00\%} (\texttt{@BBB} solo) to \texttt{60.00\%} (\texttt{@BBB} primed) and \textbf{\texttt{100.00\%}} (\texttt{@LUMEN} primed and 3-Model Dual-Seat Stack), with \texttt{5.64--19.92\,ms} (mean \texttt{10.52\,ms}) cortical arena routing and \texttt{2.05--32.50\,s} end-to-end turn verification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction \& Principal Contributions}
Deploying autonomous AI agents over multi-million-word enterprise and scientific codebases exposes a fundamental tension between \textit{parametric reasoning} and \textit{episodic/factual storage}. Monolithic cloud LLMs force both functions into a single volatile GPU KV-cache, resulting in 8--25\,s Time-to-First-Token (TTFT) latencies and attention dilution \cite{liu2024lost}. Conversely, 4B--9B local models fit comfortably within consumer 12\,GB VRAM envelopes and offer rapid token generation, but lack the raw parametric capacity to store millions of domain-specific facts and edge-case failure modes.

Following Geva et al. \cite{geva2021transformer}, approximately two-thirds of a transformer's parameters reside in Feed-Forward Network (FFN) layers that operate as associative key-value memories. BAMA externalizes this associative key-value substrate into locked bare-metal DDR5 RAM (\texttt{VirtualLock}) and NVMe Write-Ahead Logs (WAL), preserving 100\% of the local model's active attention heads inside a lean 16k-token L1 execution register.

\paragraph{Summary of Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{90/10 NBCW Split \& Dual-Cap B.I.F.R.O.S.T. Packing:} We replace monolithic long contexts with a 10\% L1 Active Execution Register and a 90\%+ content-addressed cortical graph, achieving \texttt{97.66\%} mean bin utilization across the 10.35M-word BAMA Flagship corpus (46 content bins + \texttt{BIN\_00}) and \texttt{139,764 words/sec} unique-word ingestion throughput (\texttt{uniqueWordsPacked = 26,211,318} / \texttt{ingestElapsedSec = 187.54\,s} in \texttt{BEACON\_HQ\_SUMMARY.json}, or \texttt{141,954 words/sec} for \texttt{totalBinWords = 26,622,069} / \texttt{187.54\,s} across 127 bins in \texttt{BIFROST\_BINS\_MANIFEST.json}) on the Beacon HQ corpus.
    \item \textbf{4-Tier Cognitive Compilation ($W_1$--$W_4$) \& Fail-Closed Governance:} We compile unstructured prose into $W_1$ Facts, $W_2$ Procedural Circuits, $W_3$ Negative Traps, and $W_4$ Affective Anchors governed by the P-CANS 5-signal gate and \texttt{owner\_agent != verifier\_agent} executable receipts.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Decompression 256-Byte \texttt{ZDC-QEGG} Cells in $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$:} We encode 80 cortical kernels into \texttt{20,480 bytes} (4 CPU cache lines per cell; \texttt{12.0$\times$} smaller than 768-D Float32 and \texttt{6.0$\times$} smaller than FP16) using golden integer ring arithmetic, eliminating float decompression buffers (\texttt{0\,B}) and achieving \texttt{0.0608\,ms} multi-modal Matryoshka LUT lookups.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Ablations \& 8-Tier Task Evaluation:} We provide complete component ablations (Tiers 1--15, \texttt{66/66} unit tests) and evaluate local 4B/9B models across a 5-task author-written verification suite (15 deterministic sub-assertions; unprimed baseline receipt \texttt{BAMA\_HONEST\_UNPRIMED\_INDUSTRY\_BENCHMARK.json} and primed receipt \texttt{BAMA\_BLIND\_PRIMED\_72KERNEL\_BENCHMARK.json}, \texttt{2026-10-20T08:43:42Z}), transparently contrasting out-of-domain un-primed recall (\texttt{33.34\%--46.66\%}) against in-domain primed + $W_3$-verified execution (\texttt{60.00\%--100.00\%}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related Work \& Architectural Positioning}
Existing external memory architectures fall into three categories:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Virtual Context Paging:} \textit{MemGPT / Letta} \cite{packer2023memgpt} and \textit{Mem0} \cite{chhikara2025mem0} page historical context between active prompts and vector/graph databases via LLM tool calls. While effective for conversational continuity, each memory operation invokes LLM inference (\texttt{180\,ms--2.5\,s} architectural range) and risks lossy prose summarization.
    \item \textbf{Temporal \& Community Knowledge Graphs:} \textit{Zep / Graphiti} \cite{rasmussen2025zep} tracks bi-temporal entity subgraphs, while \textit{Microsoft GraphRAG} \cite{edge2024graphrag} builds Leiden community hierarchies. However, GraphRAG requires expensive map-reduce LLM synthesis at query time (\texttt{1.5--12\,s}), and unpenalized graph walks suffer from super-node hub dominance.
    \item \textbf{Externalized Key-Value \& Explicit Memory:} \textit{RETRO} \cite{borgeaud2022retro} and \textit{Memory$^3$} \cite{yang2024memory3} externalize knowledge into retrieval chunks or explicit sparse KV tensors based on FFN key-value duality \cite{geva2021transformer}. BAMA brings this paradigm to unmodified local GGUF/Ollama models via zero-decompression integer-ring graph cells in locked DDR5 RAM.
\end{itemize}

\section{The 90/10 NBCW Split, Dual-Cap B.I.F.R.O.S.T., and $W_1$--$W_4$ Compilation}
To prevent ``Lost in the Middle'' attention degradation \cite{liu2024lost}, BAMA pins the active prompt at $\le 16,384$ tokens (the \textbf{10\% L1 Execution Register}) and offloads 90\%+ of corpus state into content-addressed \textbf{Dual-Cap B.I.F.R.O.S.T.} bins enforced by First-Fit Decreasing (FFD) bin packing:
\begin{equation}
\forall k \in \{01,\dots,N\}:\quad \text{Words}(B_k) \le 265{,}000 \;\wedge\; \text{Bytes}(B_k) \le 2.05\,\text{MB}
\end{equation}
with \texttt{BIN\_00} reserved as an $\mathcal{O}(1)$ Master Routing Index. Offline compilation transforms raw prose into four typed cortical matrices: $W_1$ (Epistemic Facts), $W_2$ (Procedural Circuits), $W_3$ (Negative Constraint \& Anti-Hallucination Traps), and $W_4$ (Micro-LoRA / Relational Anchors).

\subsection{Built Arenas vs.\ Theoretical 1\,TiB FFN Key-Value Capacity Projection}
We distinguish our \textbf{built and measured cortical arenas} (\texttt{13,568\,B} for 53 kernels, \texttt{18,432\,B} for 72 kernels, and \texttt{20,480\,B} for 80 kernels, plus the \texttt{1.84\,GB} Beacon HQ / BAMA Flagship DDR5 index over \texttt{36.55M} words) from the analytical upper-bound capacity model:
\begin{equation}
C_{\text{eff}}(M_{\text{local}} \oplus \mathcal{G}_{\text{BAMA}}) = P_{\text{dense}}(M_{\text{local}}) + \frac{N_{\text{cells}} \cdot D_{\text{trits}} \cdot \log_2(3) \cdot \eta_{\text{compile}} \cdot \rho_{\text{PPR}}(\theta)}{\beta_{\text{param}}}
\end{equation}
where $D_{\text{trits}}=768$, $\log_2(3)\approx 1.585$\,bits/trit, and $\beta_{\text{param}}\approx 2.0$\,bits/parameter (following Allen-Zhu \& Li's capacity law). Our built 80-kernel arena ($N_{\text{cells}}=80$) stores $97,380$ raw trinary bits. A hypothetical 1\,TiB address space ($N_{\text{cells}}=2^{32}$) would store $5.23\times 10^{12}$ trinary bits ($\approx 2.61\text{T}$ raw 2-bit parameter equivalents before structural pruning factors $\eta_{\text{compile}} \in [2.15, 3.74]$, yielding the \texttt{5.62T--9.78T} analytical projection in \texttt{bama\_1tb\_equivalence\_proof.py}). We emphasize that compression does not multiply Shannon entropy, no 1\,TiB cell graph was populated in this study, and externalized FFN lookup does not replace dense gradient backpropagation on unindexed domains.

\section{Zero-Decompression \texttt{ZDC-QEGG} Cells in $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ \& GAAMA 2-Hop PPR}
Standard 768-D embeddings require $3,072$\,bytes per node in \texttt{float32} ($245,760$\,B for 80 kernels) or $1,536$\,bytes in \texttt{float16} ($122,880$\,B for 80 kernels). BAMA encodes each cortical kernel into an exact \textbf{256-byte (4 CPU cache-line aligned) \texttt{ZDC-QEGG} cell} over the real golden quadratic integer ring $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi] = \{a + b\varphi \mid a,b \in \mathbb{Z}\} \subset \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$, where $\varphi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ and $\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$ (retaining the legacy codebase identifier \texttt{QEGG} while operating in $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ rather than complex Eisenstein integers $\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Cache Line 0 (Bytes 0..63):} Kernel ID, Layer, Tier, Modality mask, and Dual-Modulus CRT residues ($p_1=251, p_2=253$).
    \item \textbf{Cache Line 1 (Bytes 64..127):} Coarse integer ring coefficients ($a_i$) supporting Matryoshka \cite{kusupati2022matryoshka} prefixes $\{128, 256\}$.
    \item \textbf{Cache Line 2 (Bytes 128..191):} Golden conjugate coefficients ($b_i \varphi$), gated by \textbf{$1/\varphi$ Lazy Child Bit-Plane Collapse} ($\tau_\varphi = \varphi^{-1} \approx 0.618034$), which skips \textbf{\texttt{61.77\%}} of child byte reads.
    \item \textbf{Cache Line 3 (Bytes 192..255):} Inline 2-Hop Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) adjacency pointers, $W_3$ trap bitmask, and 64-bit SHA-256 lineage tag.
\end{itemize}
Ring inner products $(a_1 + b_1\varphi)(a_2 + b_2\varphi) = (a_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2) + (a_1 b_2 + b_1 a_2 + b_1 b_2)\varphi$ execute directly via a \textbf{16-Entry Dual-Nibble LUT} in L1 cache with \texttt{0\,bytes} of float decompression overhead.

\subsection{P-CANS Promotion \& GAAMA Hub-Dampened PPR}
Nodes enter permanent graph memory only when their 5-signal P-CANS promotion gate fires and an independent verifier receipt confirms \texttt{owner\_agent != verifier\_agent}. Runtime retrieval in \texttt{bama\_cortical\_graph\_engine.py} and \texttt{verify\_bama\_spine.py} executes 2-Hop Personalized PageRank \cite{page1999pagerank} with teleportation probability $\alpha=0.15$ (walk retention $1-\alpha=0.85$) and degree-threshold hub dampening ($\theta=50$):
\begin{equation}
p^{(t+1)}(v) = \alpha\,p^{(0)}(v) + (1-\alpha)\sum_{u : v \in \mathcal{N}(u)} p^{(t)}(u)\,\frac{\text{damp}(\text{deg}(u))}{\text{deg}(u)}, \quad \text{damp}(d) = \min\!\left(1.0,\, \frac{\theta}{d}\right)
\end{equation}
(Tier 7 \texttt{bama\_hyperion\_tier7.py} additionally exposes an optional logarithmic dampener $1/\ln(d+2)$, and \texttt{bama\_cortical\_graph\_engine.py} multiplies each step's share by \texttt{carrier\_gain}, which equals $1.0$ on unmodulated edges.) In \texttt{verify\_bama\_spine.py} (\texttt{test\_03\_2hop\_gaama\_ppr\_hub\_dampening}), a seed node branching equally to a specific 2-hop circuit ($\text{deg}=2 \le \theta$, $\text{damp}(2)=1.0$) and a structural hub ($\text{deg}=101 > \theta$, $\text{damp}(101)=50/101 \approx 0.4950$) yields \textbf{\texttt{0.180625}} on \texttt{TARGET\_SPEC} (\#1 rank, \textbf{\texttt{102.0$\times$}} higher than \texttt{TARGET\_HUB}) while suppressing \texttt{TARGET\_HUB} by \textbf{\texttt{50.50\%}} (\texttt{0.003577} $\to$ \texttt{0.001771} at $d=101$, and \textbf{\texttt{90.00\%}} suppression at $d=500$ where $\theta/d = 0.10$).

\subsection{Closed-Book Domain-Priming Disclosure, Limitations \& Reproducibility}
We highlight three methodological boundaries: (1) \textit{Closed-Book Domain Priming vs.\ Out-of-Domain Generalization:} Kernels \texttt{K54--K58} in the 72-kernel arena (\texttt{18,432\,B}) were authored to store the first-principles axioms required by Tasks 1--5. Thus, T3a--T5a measure out-of-domain un-primed recall (\texttt{33.34\%--46.66\%}, loaded from \texttt{BAMA\_HONEST\_UNPRIMED\_INDUSTRY\_BENCHMARK.json}), whereas T3b--T5b measure in-domain cortical retrieval paired with deterministic \texttt{W3SelfVerificationTrap} execution under blind hash routing (\texttt{BAMA\_BLIND\_PRIMED\_72KERNEL\_BENCHMARK.json}). Following internal peer review, all domain-specific hints were removed from the verifier/repair prompts in \texttt{src/bama\_prime\_collective\_graph.py}: with zero verifier hints, \texttt{@BBB} Primed achieves \texttt{60.00\%}, while \texttt{@LUMEN} Primed and the 3-Model Dual-Seat Stack achieve \texttt{100.00\%} (with \texttt{@LUMEN} passing Task 4 on its first draft in \texttt{6.98\,s}). Expanding evaluation to full upstream benchmark corpora (12,000-item MMLU-Pro, 198-item GPQA-Diamond, 500-item MATH) is scheduled for our MLSys 2027 submission. (2) \textit{Arena Routing vs.\ Subprocess Turn Latency:} Binary arena routing requires \texttt{5.64--19.92\,ms} (mean \texttt{10.52\,ms}), while total turn latency including local LLM generation and subprocess sandbox execution is \texttt{2.05--32.50\,s}. (3) \textit{Reproducibility:} All 15 architectural tiers are validated by \textbf{\texttt{66/66} (100\%) passing unit tests} executing in $<2.5$\,s.
